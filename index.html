EFTarkov Buddy

The ultimate companion app for Escape From Tarkov. Track your side quests, 1.0 main story, Hideout modules, and physical items.

🚀 Download for Windows (v1.0.0)

📜 Quest & Main Story Tracker

Keep track of all side quests and 1.0 main story chapters filtered by trader and map.

🏠 Hideout Manager

Manage requirements for all Hideout modules seamlessly.

📦 Item Tracker

Smart filtering system for physical items needed for quests and module upgrades.

🗺️ Maps & Keys

Interactive high-res map view with essential keys list and official Wiki integration.
