// 01 — CAPABILITIES

Everything your PMC needs,
one window away.

Stop alt-tabbing to a dozen wiki pages. Buddy keeps your progress, requirements and maps in a single tactical dashboard.

01

Quest & Main Story Tracker

A complete database of side quests and the 1.0 main story chapters, organized by trader and by map so you always know where to go next.

02

Hideout Module Manager

Full control of upgrade paths and module requirements, so you level up your hideout efficiently instead of guessing what's next.

03

Smart Item Tracker

Automatically totals every physical item needed for pending quests and hideout upgrades — filtering out objective action verbs, so you see only what to loot.

04

High-Res Interactive Maps

Detailed map locations alongside essential key lists, with direct links to the official Tarkov Wiki when you need the full picture.

05

PMC Profile & Traders

Your level, loyalty levels and reputation across every trader, centralized in one dashboard.

06

Backups & Wipe Reset

Export and import your progress as JSON backups, or trigger a full wipe / prestige reset when a new season starts.
